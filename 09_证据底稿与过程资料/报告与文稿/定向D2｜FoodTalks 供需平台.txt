# FoodTalks 供需平台

FoodTalks供需平台（www.foodtalks.cn/wefood）是食品行业供需发布与社交平台，于2020年9月上线运营。平台提供食品配料、代工、包装、设计、营销、招商和设备等公司的供需信息，目前2w+公司发布了4w+条供需信息，注册人数10w+人。我们希望致力于打通食品行业信息链，实现企业高效对接！

# FoodTalks
## FoodTalks B2B Platform

FoodTalks B2B Platform ([www.foodtalks.cn/wefood](https://www.foodtalks.cn/wefood)) gathers people in the food industry and provides free supply and demand information posts. It was officially launched in September, 2020.

The platform provides supply and demand information of ingredients, OEM, packaging, design, marketing, investment promotion and equipment companies in the food industry.

At present, more than 20,000 companies have released over 40,000 supply and demand informations on our platform, and the number of registrations of our platform has reached 100k+. We are committed to opening up the information chain of the food industry, and achieving the efficient connection of enterprises!

### Platform Statistics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Number of posts | 60k+ |
| Number of registrants | 100k+ |
| Number of companies | 20k+ |

**Data sampling:** October 2020 – May 2024  
**Data source:** FoodTalks Admin Site
